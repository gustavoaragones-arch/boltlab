Tap Drill Calculator

Quick answer

Tap drill size is the pilot hole diameter you drill before cutting internal threads with a tap.

It is smaller than major diameter so taps form enough thread height without over-torquing.

Use the calculator below for metric sizes, then cross-check inch pilots with tap drill charts when needed.

Bolt sizing and thread specifications follow widely recognized mechanical standards used in engineering and manufacturing. Standard bolt sizing references, industrial fastener charts, and mechanical thread standards ensure compatibility, safety, and correct tool selection across applications.

Calculation method

For metric taps, a common workshop formula is major diameter minus pitch. This tool also shows coarse reference values from a lookup table. Cross-check unknown threads with the thread identifier before you tap critical parts.

Always confirm the final tap-drill size against your tap manufacturer's chart or technical data.

FAQ

How do you calculate tap drill size?

A common metric approximation is major diameter minus thread pitch. For example, M6 x 1.0 gives about 5.0 mm tap drill.

Does this tool include common metric thread sizes?

Yes. It includes M3, M4, M5, M6, M8, and M10 with coarse reference values.
