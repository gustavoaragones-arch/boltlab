    Hole preparation

    BoltLab's primary tap-drill value represents the pilot-hole diameter — the hole drilled prior to tapping. Hole geometry (through vs. blind) and hole function (tapped vs. clearance) are modeled in BoltLab's knowledge layer as separate, non-exclusive concepts, not as per-thread-size data available in this dataset today, so they are not offered here as a selectable filter.
