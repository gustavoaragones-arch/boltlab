ASME Thread and Tap Standards

ASME B1.1 unified inch thread system and ASME B94.9 tap tool nomenclature.

BoltLab references two ASME standards directly relevant to its own inch-thread and tapping data: ASME B1.1 (Unified Inch Screw Threads (UN, UNR, and UNJ Thread Forms)) and ASME B94.9 (Taps: Ground Thread with Cut Thread Appendix (Inch and Metric Sizes)).

Use related standards and concepts to connect profile geometry, designation planning, dimensions, and tolerance interpretation.

Use BoltLab tools and charts for workflow interpretation while final release values come from approved standards documents.

What This Standard Covers

  • ASME B1.1: Defines unified inch thread form, series, class, allowance, tolerance, and designation for UN, UNR, and UNJ thread forms. UNJ (formerly covered by the separate ASME B1.15) has been incorporated into B1.1 since the 2019 edition.
  • ASME B94.9: Covers tap designs, nomenclature, definitions, marking system, and dimension/tolerance tables for standard tap types and styles, including straight-fluted, spiral-fluted, spiral-point, and thread-forming taps, in inch and metric sizes.

When Engineers Use It

  • Interpreting the UNC/UNF designation, series, allowance, and tolerance conventions that BoltLab's own unc_threads/unf_threads datasets follow, per ASME B1.1 (Referenced for UNC/UNF system interpretation and inch-thread engineering context. Current edition confirmed as ASME B1.1-2024.)
  • Understanding the tap tool nomenclature behind BoltLab's spiral-flute, spiral-point, and forming tap-type classifications, per ASME B94.9 (Reference standard for tap tool nomenclature, styles, and manufacturing dimensions/tolerances. Current edition ASME B94.9-2008, reaffirmed 2023.)
